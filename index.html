Your AI bench partner for hardware integration.

silkscreen turns your ECAD files, Gerbers and pinout documents into one assembly record, then works with your AI harness of choice to resolve conflicts before integration, guide checks at the bench, and answer any questions you have, down to every pin and wire.

Bring your own agent.

Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity drive silkscreen through the same operations as the interface. They trace signals, read the ICDs and schematics, plan bench checks and explain what a conflict does electrically.

Each chat runs in Proposals only, where every change waits for you, or Auto mode, where changes apply at once and are logged in Activity. Web lookups for datasheets stay off until you allow them.

You bring your own deployment. silkscreen drives the harness CLI you already have installed, signed in however you like: a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, API keys, or your company's own access. silkscreen never sees your credentials, and design files never leave your machine.
